FAQ

Questions

Is SnapWOD free?

Yes. SnapWOD is free to download, and you can build and run any timer for free, offline. You also get free smart reads every month. Premium adds more smart reads, more saved workouts with cloud backup, and sharing.

What's a smart read?

A smart read is when SnapWOD reads a photo, screenshot or pasted text and builds the timer for you, including multi-part sessions, sets, rounds and rest. You always see a preview first and can adjust any time before you start.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use SnapWOD as a guest, including smart reads and buying Premium. Signing in with Apple, Google or email is optional and keeps your Premium and saved workouts across devices.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Timers run without a connection, and device mode reads simple workouts offline. Smart mode needs an internet connection.

Will the timer keep going when my phone locks?

Yes. The countdown keeps running on your lock screen with 3-2-1 beeps, and on iPhone it also shows in the Dynamic Island and on a paired Apple Watch.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 16.0 or later, and Android phones via Google Play.

Is SnapWOD affiliated with CrossFit or HYROX?

No. SnapWOD is an independent app for people who do CrossFit-style and HYROX-style training. CrossFit and HYROX are trademarks of their respective owners.

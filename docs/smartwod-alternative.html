Which one suits you

Pick SmartWOD if

you want a well-established timer app with a long track record and a lot of ratings, you set your own workouts up and you already cast to a TV with Chromecast.

Pick a wall clock if

you want a timer that's always on the wall, readable from across the room, and never depends on a phone, wi-fi or a TV being switched on. The trade-off is the price per clock and that it only shows numbers.

Pick SnapWOD if

the workout is already written on the whiteboard and you don't want to set every interval up by hand, or you want members to see the movements for each minute on the TV rather than walking over to the board. It's free, and the TV needs only a web browser.

For coaches and gym owners

  • Use the TV you already have. Smart TV, streaming stick or a laptop on HDMI: anything with a browser.
  • Run the class from your phone. Start, pause and skip on the phone; the TV follows.
  • Open gym too. Members put their own workout on the TV the same way, from their own phone.
  • Free for coaches. Coaches at gyms running SnapWOD on the TV get Premium at no cost: email admin@snapwod.co.uk.

Questions

Is SnapWOD a free alternative to SmartWOD?

Yes. SnapWOD is free on iPhone and Android. Premium is optional and adds more smart whiteboard reads and cloud backup.

Do I need a Chromecast or a TV app?

No. The TV only needs a web browser. Open snapwod.co.uk/tv, type the code shown on the phone, and the phone runs the timer.

Can SnapWOD replace a wall clock?

If the gym has a TV or monitor, yes: it shows the clock, the round and the movements. A wall clock is still the better pick if you want something that works with no phone, wi-fi or TV.

Can members use it in open gym?

Yes. Any member can put their own workout on the TV from their own phone with the same code system.

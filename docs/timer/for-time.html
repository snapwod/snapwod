How For Time works

A For Time workout is a fixed amount of work done as fast as possible. Your score is the time on the clock when you finish, so lower is better. Most gyms add a time cap: if you are still going when the cap is reached, you stop and record the reps you completed instead.

This timer counts up from zero after the get-ready countdown. Tap Done when you finish and the clock freezes on your time. If you reach the cap, it stops by itself with a long beep.

Setting a time cap

A good cap lets most people finish while stopping anyone who would otherwise be there all day. A rule of thumb is about one and a half times the time you expect a solid athlete to take.

  • Short couplets like 21-15-9: 8 to 10 minutes.
  • Three to five rounds with a run: 15 to 25 minutes.
  • Long chippers and Hero workouts: 40 to 60 minutes.

Pacing tips

Plan your set breaks before you start, write them on the board and stick to them. On a short workout, the second round is usually where time is won or lost: keep moving even when the reps get small.

Whiteboard to timer

Take a photo of the gym whiteboard, or paste the workout from your gym's app, and SnapWOD builds the timer for you. No programming every interval by hand.

How it works

  1. Snap the board. Open SnapWOD and take a photo of the whiteboard. Copied the workout from your gym's app or a programming site? Paste the text instead.
  2. Check the preview. SnapWOD shows each part it found, with the time, the rounds and the rest. If something's off, take the photo again or type the workout in.
  3. Start the clock. The timer beeps you through every part and keeps running with your phone locked. Coaching a class? Put it on the gym TV.

An example

On the whiteboard

A) EMOM 12
1: 12 cal row
2: 10 burpees
3: 15 wall balls

Rest 3:00

B) AMRAP 10
5 pull-ups
10 push-ups
15 air squats

The timer SnapWOD builds

  1. EMOM 12:00, 12 one-minute rounds rotating through the row, the burpees and the wall balls, with each minute's movement on screen.
  2. Rest 3:00, counted down, with a beep before part B.
  3. AMRAP 10:00, with the three movements on screen.

What it reads

  • EMOM, including E2MOM and E3MOM, and alternating stations minute by minute.
  • AMRAP of any length.
  • For Time, with the time cap if the board has one.
  • Intervals and Tabata: work and rest times, rounds and sets.
  • Multi-part sessions: a strength piece, then a conditioning piece, with the rest between them built in.

Smart read and the basic reader

SnapWOD has two ways to read a workout.

Smart read handles the whole whiteboard: several parts, the movements for each minute, rest between parts, and messy handwriting. You get 2 smart reads a month free, and up to 100 a month with Premium.

The basic reader runs on your phone, free and unlimited, and works offline. It is good for a single format with the time written clearly, such as "EMOM 12" or "AMRAP 20". For a session with several parts, use a smart read.

Either way, check the preview before you start. Clear block capitals read best; a photo taken square-on, without glare, reads better than one from across the room.

On the gym TV

Coaches can put the timer on any TV with a web browser. Open snapwod.co.uk/tv on the TV, type the six-letter code from the phone, and run the class from the phone. The TV shows the clock, the movements for the current part, the round and what's next. Nothing to install on the TV. See how it compares with a timer app or a wall clock.

Just need a quick timer?

The free web timer runs EMOM, AMRAP, For Time, Tabata and intervals in your browser with no install. The workout library has the benchmark WODs ready to run.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. SnapWOD is free on iPhone and Android, with 2 smart reads a month and the basic reader without limits. Premium adds up to 100 smart reads a month and cloud backup for up to 30 saved workouts.

Can it read handwriting?

Yes, with a smart read. Clear block capitals work best. Check the preview before you start, and take the photo again if something was misread.

Can I paste a workout from my gym's app?

Yes. Copy the workout text from your gym's app, website or a programming page and paste it into SnapWOD instead of taking a photo.

Does it work offline?

The timer and the basic reader work offline. A smart read needs an internet connection.

Which formats does it understand?

EMOM (including E2MOM and E3MOM), AMRAP, For Time with a cap, intervals, Tabata and multi-part sessions with rest between the parts.
